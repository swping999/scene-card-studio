# Example asset provenance and usage

All raster images under `examples/photos/`, `examples/cases/*/photos/`, and
`examples/cases/*/outputs/` were generated specifically for Scene Card Studio
with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-08-09. They depict
fictional scenes and people and were not derived from user photographs.

The project author makes these repository-specific demo images available under

them. Their inclusion does not grant rights in third-party trademarks or other
material that may incidentally appear.

Deterministic PNG and SVG workprints under `examples/outputs/` are generated
from this project's code and the same repository-specific source images and
are distributed under the repository's Apache License 2.0.

The bundled font under `src/moments_to_pages/assets/fonts/` is excluded from
the Apache-2.0 grant and remains licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Its complete notice and license text are stored beside the font.

The bundled memory-map illustration under
`src/moments_to_pages/assets/maps/` was generated specifically for this
repository on 2026-08-09 and is distributed under the repository's Apache
License 2.0.

The 26 JPEG files under `examples/cases/v0.4-gallery/before/` and
`examples/cases/v0.4-gallery/after/`, together with the opening media under
`docs/media/`, were added on 2026-08-10. The Before/After images were generated
specifically for this repository with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool;
they depict fictional people, places, and objects and were not derived from
user photographs or third-party visual assets. The opening MP4 was supplied by
the project author, and the GIF and poster are local derivatives of that MP4.
These assets are available under the same repository-specific Apache-2.0 grant
described above, to the extent the project author holds rights in them.
